Step 2 · Atoms and molecules

An atom is a basic unit of an element. A molecule is two or more atoms chemically bonded. Molecules can have matching atoms (O₂), or different atoms (H₂O). A compound contains two or more different elements chemically bonded.

Step 3 · Solutions

A solution is a homogeneous mixture. In sugar water, sugar is the solute (what dissolves) and water is the solvent (what does the dissolving).

Worked example: Is salad a compound or mixture? It is a heterogeneous mixture: its ingredients are physically combined and are not evenly distributed.

Lesson 3 · About 15 minutes

Density: mass ÷ volume

Density tells us how much mass is packed into a given volume.

Density = Mass ÷ Volume
D = m / V

Mass is measured in grams (g); volume can be measured in milliliters (mL); density can be measured in g/mL.

Step 1 · A basic example

A sample has a mass of 36 g and volume of 12 mL.
D = 36 ÷ 12 = 3 g/mL.

Step 2 · Subtract the empty beaker first

Empty beaker: 30 g
Beaker + liquid: 78 g
Liquid volume: 60 mL

Liquid mass = 78 − 30 = 48 g
Density = 48 ÷ 60 = 0.8 g/mL
Watch out: The mass of the beaker is NOT part of the liquid's mass. Subtract it before dividing.
